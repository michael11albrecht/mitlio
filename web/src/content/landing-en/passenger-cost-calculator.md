## Two views of the same lift

Passengers want to know what to offer. Drivers want to ask for an amount without it feeling awkward. The calculator answers both with the same numbers.

`distance ÷ 100 × consumption × price per litre`

## Choosing who pays

- **Driver pays a share:** total divided by everyone in the car.
- **Driver pays nothing:** total divided by the passengers only. The driver then receives the full fuel cost back.

Neither is right or wrong. Decide before the trip and the figure speaks for itself.

## Practical notes

- **No platform fees:** this tool only calculates; it does not arrange rides or take money.
- **Currency:** euros and kilometres by default, but you can read the numbers in any currency.
